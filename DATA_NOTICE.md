# Data notice

The executable examples and regression cases in this repository are synthetic.
PFMEA records, control plans, SOPs, inspection records, approvals, validation
reports, identities, signatures, and engineering-change fields were created for
software testing and demonstration.

Public NHTSA-hosted documents are referenced only as factual background for a
synthetic scenario. Those source documents are not included or relicensed by
this repository. The synthetic cases are not Toyota, supplier, customer, or
factory records and must not be treated as official remediation instructions.

Benchmark results measure reproducibility against jointly developed synthetic
expectations. They do not measure real-world manufacturing accuracy, safety,
quality improvement, recall prevention, deployment maturity, or commercial
return.

Do not add customer files, confidential supplier data, personal information,
production credentials, or proprietary engineering documents to this
repository.
